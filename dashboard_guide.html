Yeh guide aapko Boutique Ops Dashboard ke har ek option ka maksad (purpose), usme kya kaam karna hai, aur step-by-step kaise operate karna hai, aasan shabdon me samjhata hai.

📋 Quick Overview — Kis Option Me Kya Kaam Hota Hai?

Dashboard Option Asal Maksad (Purpose) Aapko Kya Kaam Karna Hai?
1. Overview & Metrics Dukaan ka live report & summary dekhna. Total revenue, pending orders, low stock items, aur recent activities ka bird's-eye view monitor karein.
2. Product Catalog Naye sarees/suits ko website par launch karna. Naya design banayein, Price set karein, Fabric & care likhein, draft ya publish status set karein.
3. Media & Gallery Saree ki photos upload & arrange karna. Photos upload karein, kaunsi photo main thumbnail (Primary) hogi wo select karein.
4. Inventory Matrix Stock count manage karna aur restock intake lena. Master weaver se naya maal aane par + Add Stock karein (1 to ∞), ya direct naya SKU add karein.
5. Sales & Campaigns Festive sale offers & discounts chalana. Vinayaka Chavithi, Diwali, Eid offers create karein, From/To dates schedule karein, live countdown timer on karein.
6. Orders & Payments Customer ke orders pack & dispatch karna. Customer address dekhein, IMPS/NEFT bank payment UTR confirm karke order dispatch/deliver mark karein.
7. Advance Orders Excusive loom reservations book karna. Jin customers ne advance token dekar exclusive saree book ki hai, stock aane par unhe saree allocate karein.
8. Settings & Tickers
(Super Admin Only)		 Brand name, address & announcements badalna. Dukaan ka naam, address, delivery radius (km), 2+1 bundle engine toggle, aur chalne wala announcement ticker manage karein.
1. Overview & Metrics Store Health
💡 Kab Use Karein?

Din shuru hote hi dukaan ki overall performance dekhne ke liye.

  • Aaj ki total sales aur earnings (₹).
  • Kitne orders pending hain jinhe pack karna hai.
  • Kaunsi saree ka stock khatam hone wala hai.
🛠️ Step-by-Step Kaam:
  1. Top metric cards par ek nazar daalein (Total Revenue, Pending Orders, Low Stock Alerts).
  2. Agar koi low-stock alert dikhe, direct Inventory Matrix me jakar restock karein.
  3. Agar pending orders dikhein, Orders tab me jakar payment check karein.
2. Product Catalog Catalog Management
💡 Kab Use Karein?

Jab aapko store me naya saree weave ya dress customer ke dekhne ke liye add/edit karna ho.

  • Naya Saree design publish karna.
  • Selling price ya Sale discount price edit karna.
  • Homepage placement set karna (Featured, Hero, New Arrival).
🛠️ Step-by-Step Kaam:
  1. + Add New Product button par click karein.
  2. Saree Name, Category (Sarees, Punjabi, etc.) aur Fabric type likhein.
  3. Cost Price (kharid rate) aur Selling Price (bikri rate) dalein. (Safeguard: Selling price kharid rate se kam nahi ho sakti).
  4. Save karein — Saree turant website par live dikhne lagegi!
3. Media & Gallery Visual Assets
💡 Kab Use Karein?

Saree ki high-resolution photos upload aur manage karne ke liye.

  • Saree ke multiple angles (Pallu, Bootee, Model drape) upload karna.
  • Kaunsi photo pehle dikhegi wo "Primary Image" select karna.
🛠️ Step-by-Step Kaam:
  1. Left list me se saree select karein.
  2. Image URL ya photo file upload karein.
  3. Jis photo ko main banner banana ho, uspar "Make Primary" click karein.
4. Inventory Matrix (Stock Management) Very Important
💡 Kab Use Karein?

Jab master loom ya weaver se naya stock godown/store me aata hai.

  • Tab 1 (Restock Existing Article): Puraane design ka stock badhana.
  • Tab 2 (+ Add New Article / SKU): Bina catalog gaye seedha nayi saree aur uska initial stock add karna.
🛠️ Step-by-Step Kaam:
  1. Top par "+ Add Inventory / Stock" button dabayein.
  2. Restock Existing: SKU chuniye → Units dalein (1 to ∞, koi limit nahi) → "Add to Stock" dabayein.
  3. Add New Article: Tab 2 dabayein → Name, SKU, Fabric, Rate, aur Stock Quantity bhariye → Save karein.
⚠️ Safeguard Note: Negative inventory add karna strictly allowed nahi hai. Quantity hamesha 1 ya usse zyada (1 to ∞) honi chahiye. Minus signs automatically block hain.
5. Sales & Campaigns (Festive Offers) Festive Engine
💡 Kab Use Karein?

Teej, Vinayaka Chavithi, Diwali, Eid, ya Wedding Season par offers chalane ke liye.

  • Discount percentage set karna (e.g. 15% ya 25% OFF).
  • Offer kab shuru hoga (From Date) aur kab band hoga (To Date).
  • Home page par live countdown timer ticker lagana.
🛠️ Step-by-Step Kaam:
  1. + New Campaign click karein.
  2. Campaign Name (e.g. "Diwali Dhamaka") aur Discount % dalein.
  3. Start Date & End Date select karein.
  4. Save karein → Home page ke Hero banner aur Festive section me automatically timer ke sath dono offers live show honge!
6. Orders & Payments Order Fulfillment
💡 Kab Use Karein?

Jab customer website par shopping karke order place karta hai.

  • Customer ka delivery address aur phone number dekhna.
  • IMPS/NEFT direct bank transfer wale orders ka UTR number check karke verify karna.
  • Order status ko "CONFIRMED" aur "DISPATCHED" mark karna.
🛠️ Step-by-Step Kaam:
  1. List me order dekhein. Agar payment IMPS hai toh UTR verify karke "Verify Payment" dabayein.
  2. Saree pack karke courier/delivery boy ko dein aur "Mark Dispatched" click karein.
7. Advance Orders (Loom Pre-Bookings) Master Weaves
💡 Kab Use Karein?

Jab koi customer bohot mehengi ya exclusive bridal saree banne se pehle token dekar reserve karta hai.

  • Customer ne kitna token advance diya hai wo record dekhna.
  • Kanchipuram ya Varanasi loom se saree tayar ho kar aane par customer ko notify karna.
🛠️ Step-by-Step Kaam:
  1. Customer ki request check karein.
  2. Jaise hi saree tayar hokar store me aaye, "Allocate Stock" button dabayein.
  3. Customer ko inform karein ki unka exclusive piece ready hai!
8. Settings & Tickers (Super Admin Only) Master Controls
💡 Kab Use Karein?

Store ki core branding, address ya announcement ticker badalne ke liye (bina code change kiye!).

  • Brand Name: Naam badalte hi poori website ka naam ek second me badal jata hai.
  • Boutique Address & Delivery Radius: Kitne km tak home delivery deni hai (e.g. 12 km).
  • 2+1 Festive Bundle Engine: "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" offer on ya off karna.
  • Homepage Ticker: Website ke top par chalne wali red/gold announcement patti.
🛠️ Step-by-Step Kaam:
  1. Brand Name, Tagline ya Address text box me edit karein.
  2. "Save Brand Settings" dabayein → Instant website update.
  3. Nayi headline chalane ke liye "Add Message" dabayein → Top ticker me live scroll hone lagega.
